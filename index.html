    How the numbers work

    1. Products are matched by exact brand and size at every store, so each comparison is like for like.
    2. The Machboos Index is the median price of one machboos plate, chicken or lamb, at a fixed list of restaurants, checked every month.
    3. The home-cooked plate uses co-op prices for 250 g rice, 300 g chicken or lamb, onion, tomato, loomi, oil and spices.
    4. Price changes compare the latest month with the same month last year.
